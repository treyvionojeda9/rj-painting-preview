FAQ

Common questions

Do you work in San Antonio?

Yes. R&J Painting and Remodeling works across the San Antonio area, including Windcrest. Call (210) 996-5459.

What kind of painting do you do?

Interior painting of walls, ceilings, trim and doors, from one room or a large living area to a whole home. Outside, we work on siding, sheds, stairs and rails.

Can you fix cracks in my walls before painting?

Yes. We repair wall cracks, including cracks from foundation movement, and repair and texture drywall before the paint goes on.

Do you remove popcorn ceilings?

Yes. Popcorn ceiling removal is part of our drywall and painting work.

Do you paint or repair kitchen cabinets?

Yes. We paint cabinets, repair broken cabinetry, and resurface counters and backsplashes.

Do you paint rental properties?

Yes. We paint rentals for property owners, including every room and new baseboards, so the place looks fresh for the next tenant.

Can you stain my fence?

Yes. Call (210) 996-5459 and tell us about the fence.

How does the estimate work?

We set up a visit and talk through your space, your expectations and your budget. Then you get a plan and a price before any work starts.

What paint do you use?

Products from Sherwin-Williams and Lowe’s, chosen to fit each project and your goals.

How can I pay?

We accept cash, check, Venmo and Zelle.
